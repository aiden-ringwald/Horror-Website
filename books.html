My Top 3 Horror Books

three books for readers who enjoy dark and unsettling stories.

BOOK 01

The Shining

Author: Stephen King

Publication Year: 1977

I love how this book is able to slowly build dread in you and make you question if what the charecters are experincing is real or not. Also managing to show how suprisingly deadly hedge animals are.

View The Shining on this Stephen King Website

BOOK 02

If It Bleeds

Author: Stephen King

Publication Year: 2020

A collection of short stories that manage to mix in supernatural elements with fear. Each storie has its own intresting atmophere to immerse yourself in.

View If It Bleeds on this Stephen King Website

BOOK 03

The Road

Author: Cormac McCarthy

Publication Year: 2006

One of the most deppressing and bleak books I have ever read, manages to show just exactly how deppressing and bleak the appocolypse would be if it happened as you follow a father and son as they walk down a Road trying to find meaning in anything at all.

View The Road on the Penguin Random House website
