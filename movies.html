My Top 4 Horror Movies

These are four horror movies I enjoy and why I recommend them.

MOVIE 01

Hereditary

Release Year: 2018

Subgenre: Horror/psychological

This movie manages to make you feel greif in way I never though possible along with the one of the most sudden and horrifying jumpscares I have every experinced this movie is a masterpeice in making you feel unsettled even ater you finish watching.

View Hereditary on IMDb

MOVIE 02

Scream

Release Year: 1996

Subgenre: Horror/slasher

Scream manages to mix both horror and comedy in a way I dont think any movie has been able to replicate. Its an amazing parody of the late 90's horror genre and is also an amazing murder mystery at the same time.

View Scream on IMDb

MOVIE 03

The Thing

Release Year: 1982

Subgenre: Horror/supernatural

Might be my personal favorite horror movie of all time. Is the most effective and engaging mystery I have seen in a horror movie along with some of the best practical effects ever done, there is a reason this movie has become such a classic even to this day.

View The Thing on IMDb

MOVIE 04

Backrooms

Release Year: 2026

Subgenre: Horror/psychological

I decide to add a recent horror movie here to show how amazing horror movies are still being made today, with some amazing charecter analysis and great practical effects, this movie has become a personal favorite for me.
