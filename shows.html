My Top 4 Horror TV Shows

Four shows worth watching when you want a longer horror story.

SHOW 01

The Twilight Zone

Release Year: 1959

Subgenre: Horror/mystery, psychological

I love just how strange and thought-provoking each episode of this manages to be. While also managing to have some of the most well known plot twist's in horror history, its amazing how this show from 70 years ago can still hold up today

View The Twilight Zone on IMDb

SHOW 02

Goosebumps

Release Year: 1995

Subgenre: horror/supernatural

While this show is less tradionally scary than a lot of the other peices of media I put on these lists, I am very nostolgic as this is the show that got me to start liking horror and im sure it has done the same for lots of other kids as well.

View Goosebumps on IMDb

SHOW 03

IT: Welcome to Derry

Release Year: 2025

Subgenre: Horror/supernatural

I love the creppy atmospher and horror that this new modern tv show is able to accomplish. Along with Bill Scarsgaurd giving an oscar worthy performace as the titular villan Pennywise, overall, this is a very good modern show to start watching horror.

View IT: Welcome to Derry on IMDb

SHOW 04

Chucky

Release Year: 2021

Subgenre: Horror/slasher

Another strong horro show and while I dont personally like slasher's as much in the horror genre, I love the way they handle chuckys charecter and is always so entertaining to watch on screen, a very soild show that I belive is worth watching.
