My Top 4 Horror Games

Four games that make horror interactive and unforgettable.

GAME 01

Alien Isolation

Release Year: 2014

Genre: Horror/Sci-Fi

I love how terrifying and unpredictable the Xenomorph feels in this game with its ability to both learn and adapt to your techinques as the game goes on makes it a horrifying to feel hunted by this alien creature.

View Alien Isolation on the playstation store

GAME 02

Resident Evil

Release Year: 1996

Genre: Horror/Survival Horror

One of the founding father of the horror genre for games and the main creator behind the idea of survival horror, this games impact alone means it has to make the list. With a bouns of being a comedy with some of the worst written lines of dialouge I have ever seen adding to the layer of comedy that other genres cant achive.

View Resident Evil on wikipedia

GAME 03

Dead Space

Release Year: 2023

Genre: Horror/Sci-Fi

I love the atmosphere in this game, how disturbing the creatures look and the feeling of isolation the game makes you feel in the emptiness of space, it truly feels like one of the worst place imaginable.

View Dead Space on EA game store

GAME 04

Signalis

Release Year: 2022

Genre: Horror/psychological

I love the errie atmosphere, and psychological horror this game manages to put on display. Along with the intential and unsettling low poly graphics which only improve the horrifying atmosphere, this is a great horror indie game I would recommend.
